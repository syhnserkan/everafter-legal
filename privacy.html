Privacy Policy – EverAfter

Effective Date: March 9, 2026

EverAfter ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application EverAfter collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

By using the EverAfter mobile application, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

EverAfter is designed to minimize data collection. We do not require account creation and we do not collect personal identity information.

However, the app may access the following information in order to function properly:

  • Photos selected by the user from the device's photo library
  • Basic device information necessary for app functionality
  • Optional analytics data (if analytics tools are used)

2. User Photos

EverAfter allows users to select photos from their device in order to generate AI-based images.

Important points regarding user photos:

  • Photos are only accessed after the user explicitly selects them
  • Photos are used solely for generating the requested AI image
  • Photos are not permanently stored by EverAfter
  • Photos are not used to train AI models

Once the generation process is completed, the original photos are not retained by the application.

3. Generated Images

Images generated by the EverAfter app:

  • Are saved only on the user's device
  • Are stored in local device storage
  • Are not uploaded to EverAfter servers
  • Are not shared with third parties

Users have full control over generated images and may delete them from their device at any time.

4. Data Storage

EverAfter does not maintain a central database of user images.

  • No user photos are stored on EverAfter servers
  • No generated images are stored on EverAfter servers
  • All generated content remains on the user's device

5. Third-Party Services

EverAfter may use limited third-party services that help operate the app, such as:

  • Analytics tools
  • AI image generation services

These services may temporarily process images in order to generate the requested output, but EverAfter does not allow them to store or reuse user content beyond the processing needed to provide the service.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information processed within the app. However, no digital service can guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for managing and securing the content stored on their own devices.

7. Children's Privacy

EverAfter is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we do, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this document. Continued use of the app after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
